Installing Melody Music

Download the file using the button above. Your browser may warn you it's not from the Play Store — that's expected for a direct APK.

Allow installs from this source if your phone asks — this is a one-time permission for apps installed outside the Play Store.

Open the downloaded file and tap Install. Melody Music will appear on your home screen once it's done.

Stay updated — Melody Music checks for new versions on launch and lets you update in a couple of taps.

Questions people ask

Why isn't Melody Music on the Play Store?

It's distributed directly as an APK, so you get the exact same app — just without going through the Play Store.

Is it safe to install an APK from outside the Play Store?

Every build is signed with the same developer key and published on the official releases page. Your phone asks for one-time permission to install from this source, which you can switch off again afterwards.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (Nougat) and newer.
