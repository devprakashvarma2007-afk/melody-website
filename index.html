Questions people ask

Why isn't Melody Music on the Play Store?

It's distributed directly as an APK instead. Downloading from this page gets you the same app, straight from the official release page on GitHub.

Is it safe to install an APK from outside the Play Store?

Every build here is signed with the same developer key and published on the official releases page. Your phone will ask for a one-time permission to install from this source, which you can turn back off afterwards.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (Nougat) and newer. Older versions won't be able to install the app.

How do I get new versions?

Melody Music checks for updates when it launches. If a newer version exists you'll get a notification and a prompt inside the app, and the download and install happen without leaving the app.

Will I lose my songs and settings when I update?

No. Favourites, downloads, playlists and your settings are stored on your device and carry over to the new version. Install the update over the existing app rather than uninstalling first.

Does it work without internet?

Downloaded songs play fully offline. Recently played tracks are cached too, so they stay available on a patchy connection. Searching for new music still needs a connection.

Where are downloaded songs saved?

In the folder you pick under Profile, defaulting to the Music folder on your device. Downloads stay put even if you clear the app's cache.

How much storage does the cache use?

The cache has a size limit and clears the oldest items on its own. You can also clear it any time from Profile, which won't touch your downloads or favourites.

Something is broken — how do I report it?

Email melodymusicsupport@gmail.com with what happened and your phone model. Bug reports and feature requests are both welcome.
